The free version

Coilbook is free to download. The free version includes the editor, unlimited code runs, the Python standard library, numpy, turtle graphics, the console and interactive console, all lessons and examples, up to 3 projects and 1 notebook you create. It isn't a trial and doesn't expire.

Coilbook Pro

Coilbook Pro is an optional in-app purchase that adds pandas and matplotlib, unlimited notebooks and projects, the Sand, Moss, Plum and Contrast editor themes, and Explain error. Explain error uses Apple Intelligence and works only on devices that support it. Pro is offered as:

  • a monthly subscription ($3.99 per month),
  • a yearly subscription ($24.99 per year), or
  • a lifetime one-time purchase ($49.99).

Prices are shown in US dollars; the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy, and that price applies. There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple, and refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

Subscriptions

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.

To manage or cancel, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone (or your account settings in the App Store). When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

Lifetime purchase

Lifetime is a one-time, non-renewing purchase that unlocks Pro for as long as you have Coilbook on a supported device. It can be shared with members of your Family Sharing group. To use it on a new device, tap Restore Purchases in Coilbook's Settings tab.

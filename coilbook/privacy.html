The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your code, notebooks and files stay on your iPhone. Python runs on the device.
  • Explain error runs on your device with Apple Intelligence. Your code isn't sent anywhere.
  • If you buy Pro, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history so your purchase works. It isn't linked to your identity or used for tracking.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Who's responsible

Coilbook is developed by Levani Topchishvili, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). This policy covers the Coilbook iPhone app and this website. Questions go to levan.topchishvili9@gmail.com.

What Coilbook doesn't collect

Coilbook has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. It doesn't collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers, and it doesn't share or sell data to anyone.

Your code and files

Scripts, notebooks, projects, lesson progress and settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside Coilbook's own storage. Projects are visible to you in the Files app under On My iPhone › Coilbook. Your code is run by the Python interpreter built into the app, on your device; it's never uploaded to me or to a server.

Like other app data, these files may be included in your iPhone backups (for example iCloud Backup) according to your own device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and I can't access them.

Coilbook doesn't install packages from the internet. If a program you write yourself uses Python's networking modules to reach the internet, that connection is under your control and outside this policy.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Coilbook Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; I never see your payment details or Apple Account information.

To check whether you have Pro and to restore purchases, Coilbook uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, and
  • your purchase history for Coilbook (which product, when, and its status, such as active or expired).

This information isn't linked to your identity and isn't used for tracking or advertising. It's used only to provide and restore your purchases. RevenueCat processes it on my behalf under its own privacy policy.

Explain error

Explain error (a Pro feature) uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models, part of Apple Intelligence. The error message and related code are processed entirely on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to me, to RevenueCat, or to any other server by Coilbook. The feature is available only on devices that support Apple Intelligence.

Information Apple provides

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give me aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports through App Store Connect. Coilbook itself contains no analytics code. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Emailing support

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you and to fix the problem you report. I don't add you to mailing lists or share your message.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers or third-party scripts. As the host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operation, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Coilbook is suitable for learners of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: delete files inside Coilbook or the Files app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Purchase records: you can ask me to delete the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat by emailing me. Your App Store purchase history itself is kept by Apple.
  • Questions or requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Since Coilbook holds almost none, most requests are simple; email me and I'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date. If a change meaningfully affects how data is handled, I'll also note it in the app's release notes.

Golf swing analyzer for iPhone and iPad

Tempoline

Film your swing in slow motion and see your tempo, checkpoint by checkpoint.

Get it on the App Store Help and FAQ

Free to download. For iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later.

What it does

Film or import

Record in slow motion at 60, 120 or 240 fps, depending on your device, with a 3, 5 or 10 second countdown. Recording stops by itself after 8 seconds. A framing outline shows where to stand, and it mirrors for left-handed golfers. You can also bring in a video from Photos or Files.

Four checkpoints, one number

Tempoline marks address, top of the backswing, impact and finish from the motion in the video, refined by on-device body tracking when you are clearly visible. Tempo is backswing time divided by downswing time, shown like 3.1 : 1. Any checkpoint can be moved.

Analyzer Free

Step frame by frame, play at 1x, 1/2, 1/4 or 1/8 speed, and scrub with checkpoint ticks on the timeline. Draw lines, angles with degrees, circles and freehand marks in five colours, with undo and clear.

Compare

Put two swings side by side, both starting from address, and mirror one to compare a left-handed and a right-handed swing. With Pro, Synced lines up address, top, impact and finish, and Overlay lays one swing semi-transparent over the other.

Tempo tab

Your average tempo over the last 10 swings, your best swing and averages per club. A three-tone tempo trainer plays tones at takeaway, top and impact, at 2 : 1, 2.5 : 1 or 3 : 1, with a backswing from 0.6 to 1.2 seconds.

Pro Pro

Body lines over the video (shoulders, hips, knees, spine), hand path, the tempo trend chart, Synced and Overlay compare, and exports of the video with lines or a single frame without the Tempoline watermark.

Help and FAQ

Short answers to the questions that come up most. For longer walkthroughs, see the guides.

A checkpoint looks a frame or two off. How do I move it?

Checkpoints are placed from the motion in the video and then refined with on-device body tracking when you are clearly visible. Motion blur, a low frame rate, poor light or something else moving in the shot (a person walking past, a swaying branch) can nudge a checkpoint a frame early or late.

To correct it, step to the frame you want, then long-press the checkpoint's chip and set it to the current frame. You can also open the Tempo sheet and tap one of its time buttons, such as "Use 1.53 s". The tempo number updates straight away.

Where should I put the camera: face-on or down the line?

Face-on: the camera faces your chest, square to the target line, roughly opposite the ball. Down the line: the camera sits behind you, looking along the target line toward the target, pointed at your hands.

For either view, put the lens at about hip height and about 3 metres (10 feet) away, so your whole body and the club stay in frame at the top and at the finish. You do not need landscape: hold the phone in portrait. Use a tripod or prop the phone on something steady. Set your usual camera angle in Settings.

The guide How to film your golf swing with a phone goes into more detail.

Which frame rate should I use?

Use the highest rate your device offers when you have good light, usually 240 fps outdoors. At 240 fps each frame is about 4 milliseconds, so checkpoints and tempo are as precise as possible. Indoors or in dim light, 120 fps gives a brighter, cleaner picture. 60 fps works, but each frame covers about 17 milliseconds and the club will blur more through impact.

The rates available depend on your iPhone or iPad. Pick your default in Settings.

Why are body lines unavailable for some swings?

Body lines (shoulders, hips, knees, spine) are a Pro feature, and they need body tracking to find you clearly in the video. They may not appear when part of your body is outside the frame, when you are very small in the picture, when the light is poor or you are backlit, or when something blocks the view of you. Checkpoints and tempo still work from the motion alone.

Refilming with your whole body in frame, in even light and from about 3 metres away usually fixes it.

I play left-handed. Does Tempoline support that?

Yes. In Settings, set handedness to Left. The framing outline on the capture screen mirrors to match your stance. In Compare, the mirror button flips one swing so you can line up a left-handed swing against a right-handed one.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings in Tempoline and tap Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. This works for both the yearly subscription and the one-time Pro for good purchase, including on a new device.

How do I cancel the free trial or the subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, tap your name (Apple Account), then Subscriptions, choose Tempoline and tap Cancel Subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Where are my swing videos stored?

On your device, inside the app. Analysis runs on the device and nothing is uploaded. A video leaves your device only when you share or export it yourself with the share sheet, for example by choosing Save Video to put a copy in Photos.

Deleting a swing deletes its video, and deleting the app deletes all swings. Export any swing you want to keep before removing the app. More in the privacy policy.

Can I analyze a video I filmed with the Camera app?

Yes. Import it from Photos or Files. A clip shot in the Camera app's slow-motion mode gives finer timing than a normal-speed clip, because there are more frames between the top of the backswing and impact.

What does the tempo number mean?

It is backswing time divided by downswing time. A tempo of 3.0 : 1 means the club took three times as long to go back as to come down to impact. The ratio describes rhythm, not speed. The guide How to measure golf swing tempo explains how to read it.

I deleted the example swings. Can I get them back?

Yes. In Settings, tap "Add the included examples again".

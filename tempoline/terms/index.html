2. What the app provides

Tempoline lets you film or import golf swing videos, marks checkpoints automatically, calculates a tempo ratio, and gives you tools to review, draw on, compare and export swings. Many features are free. Some are available only with Pro, as described in the app. Features may change over time as the app is updated.

Automatic checkpoints, tempo figures and body lines are estimates made from video. They can be off by a frame or more, especially in poor light or when you are not fully visible, and you can adjust checkpoints yourself.

7. Use it safely

  • When filming, set the phone up where neither it nor anyone holding it can be hit. Never stand, or ask someone to stand, in or near the path of the club or the ball.
  • Check the area around you before every swing, and make sure no one is walking into your swing while you watch the countdown.
  • Follow the rules of the course, range or facility, including any rules on filming and on filming other people.
  • Only film or import videos of other people with their permission.

Privacy Policy

Your swings stay on your phone or iPad. This page explains exactly what that means, and the one small exception that comes with buying Pro.

Effective 6 October 2026. Applies to the Tempoline Golf Swing Analyzer app for iPhone and iPad.

The short version

Swing videos
Kept on your device, inside the app. Never uploaded.
Checkpoints, drawings, settings
Kept on your device, inside the app. Never uploaded.
Swing analysis
Motion analysis and body tracking run on your device.
Purchases
Handled by Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat sees your purchase history and a random identifier made by the app, nothing that names you.
Not in the app
Accounts or sign-in, analytics SDKs, advertising, tracking across apps or websites.

Who is responsible

Tempoline is made by Levani Topchishvili, an individual developer based in Georgia. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean the developer, and "you" means the person using the app. If you have a question about anything here, write to levan.topchishvili@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Tempoline lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad: the swing videos you film or import, the checkpoints (address, top, impact, finish) and tempo figures, the lines, angles, circles and freehand marks you draw, and your settings such as handedness, frame rate and usual club.

When Tempoline analyses a swing, the work happens on the device. Movement in the video is measured locally, and body tracking uses Apple's Vision framework, which also runs locally. No frame, video, measurement or drawing is sent to me or to any server.

I cannot see your swings, and I have no copy of them. That also means I cannot recover them for you if they are deleted.

When a video does leave your device

Only when you send it. If you export a swing (a video with lines or a single frame) and choose a destination in the share sheet, such as Messages, Mail, AirDrop or Save Video, the file goes where you send it. What happens to it after that is governed by the app or service you chose, not by Tempoline.

Camera and photo library

Camera. Tempoline uses the camera only while the capture screen is open, to film your swing. When you leave that screen, the camera is no longer in use.

Photo library. To import a swing, you choose a video with the system picker, and Tempoline reads only that video. The app does not browse the rest of your library. Tempoline adds something to Photos only if you choose Save Video in the share sheet.

You can change camera and photo permissions at any time in the Settings app on your device.

Buying Pro

Payments are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. I never see your name, email address or payment details.

To know whether Pro is unlocked, and to restore it on another device, Tempoline uses RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase history for Tempoline and a random, anonymous app user identifier that the app generates. That identifier is not linked to who you are and is not used for tracking. It is used only to unlock Pro and to restore purchases. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy on their site.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics SDK and does not measure how you use it.
  • It shows no advertising.
  • It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • It does not sell data about you to anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a swing in the app and its video is deleted with it. Delete the app and everything it stored on the device goes too. If you want to keep a swing, export it first.

Children

Tempoline is not directed at children. The app does not collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the way Tempoline handles data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change with it. The App Store privacy information would be updated to match.

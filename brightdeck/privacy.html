Privacy policy

Short version: your decks stay on your device and I never see them. Here is the longer version.

Effective 6 October 2026

Who is behind this

Brightdeck is made by Levani Topchishvili, an individual developer. When this page says "I", that is me. You can reach me at levan.topchishvili@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your decks, cards, pictures, sounds and full review history are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account to create, and I do not run any servers that receive your cards or your study activity. I cannot see your decks, and I could not give them to anyone even if asked.

When you import an Anki package, a CSV or TSV file or pasted text, the file is read on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

The daily reminder is a local notification. Your device schedules it; nothing is sent to a server to make it happen.

What I do not collect

Brightdeck has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party ad or marketing tools. I do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers for my own purposes, and I do not build profiles of anyone.

Purchases

Brightdeck Pro purchases go through Apple. Apple handles your payment and sees your Apple ID details under its own privacy policy; I do not receive your card number or name.

To find out whether Pro is active, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app, and it receives no name or email address. It is used only to check your Pro status and restore purchases. RevenueCat has its own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Backups and deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, deleting the app deletes your decks and review history. Version 1.0 has no cloud sync, so if your cards matter to you, keep the original files you imported or use Pro to export your decks as .apkg or CSV and store them somewhere safe.

There is nothing on my side to delete, since I hold no copy of your data. If you want RevenueCat's anonymous purchase record handled, email me and I will pass the request along.

Children

Brightdeck is a study tool and is not aimed specifically at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

This website

This site is plain static pages. It sets no cookies, loads no scripts, fonts or styles from other sites and uses no analytics. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may keep ordinary server logs of visits under its own policy.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top of this page. Questions about privacy are welcome at levan.topchishvili@gmail.com.

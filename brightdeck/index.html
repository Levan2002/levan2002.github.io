Flashcards that show up right before you forget

Brightdeck is a flashcard app for iPhone and iPad. It schedules every card with FSRS spaced repetition, brings in your Anki decks, and counts down to your exam. Studying is free, with no daily limits, no ads and no account.

What it does

FSRS scheduling

Each card gets its own memory estimate. You rate Again, Hard, Good or Easy and the next review lands when you would still remember it with your chosen probability (90% by default, 80 to 97% per deck).

Exam mode

Give a deck an exam date. Intervals are capped so every card returns before the exam, and the deck shows an exam readiness percentage.

Five card types

Basic, Both ways, Type the answer, Fill the blank and Image occlusion (Pro). Typed answers are checked letter by letter, and a miss that is only case or accents shows as "almost".

Pictures and sound

Put pictures on cards, play sounds that come with imported decks, or have cards read aloud in the device's voices, with a language per deck, auto-read and a reading speed setting.

Import your decks

Anki .apkg and .colpkg packages, CSV or TSV files, and text pasted from Quizlet's Export. Open files straight from the Files app or the share sheet.

Daily reminder

An optional notification at the time you pick. It is scheduled on your device.

Want to look around first? Example decks are included: Spanish verbs, biology, US history, MCAT biochemistry, world geography and solar system labels.

Free to study, Pro if you want more

Everything you need to study is free. Brightdeck Pro is optional and adds:

  • Insights: retention over the last 30 days, a 30-day review forecast, a 16-week study calendar and your hardest cards.
  • Image occlusion: cover labels on a picture and uncover them one by one.
  • Export: save decks as .apkg (keeps your progress and imports back into Anki) or CSV.

$11.99 per year (auto-renewing) or $29.99 once for lifetime access with Family Sharing. No free trial.

Questions

Is studying really free?

Yes. You can study as much as you like with no daily limits, no ads, no account and no sign-up. Brightdeck Pro is optional and adds Insights, image occlusion and deck export.

What does Brightdeck Pro cost?

$11.99 per year (auto-renewing) or $29.99 once for lifetime access, which works with Family Sharing. There is no free trial. If you reinstall or switch devices, use Restore Purchases in Settings.

Can I bring my Anki decks?

Yes. Brightdeck opens .apkg and .colpkg packages exported from Anki, including pictures, sounds and, if you want, your review progress. See the Anki import guide.

How do I move cards from Quizlet?

Use Quizlet's Export option to get the term and definition text, then paste it into Brightdeck and choose the matching separator. The Quizlet guide walks through it.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Not in version 1.0. Everything is stored on the device it was created on. Pro can export a deck as .apkg or CSV, which is a handy backup and a way to move a deck to another device.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and we run no servers for your cards. Deleting the app deletes your decks, so keep an export or your original files as a backup. Details are in the privacy policy.

What is exam mode?

Set an exam date on a deck and Brightdeck caps review intervals so every card comes back before that day. The deck then shows an exam readiness percentage, an estimate of how many cards you will remember on exam day.

Is Brightdeck affiliated with Anki or Quizlet?

No. Anki and Quizlet are trademarks of their respective owners. Brightdeck is an independent app that can read files exported from them.

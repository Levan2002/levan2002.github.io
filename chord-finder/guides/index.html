Chord Finder guides

Short, practical guides for musicians who want to work out a song. They're written to be useful on their own, and each one ends with how to do the same thing in Chord Finder, an iPhone app that finds the chords, key and tempo of any song, video or voice memo.

All guides

  • How to find the chords of a songA step-by-step way to work out any song's chords by ear, the mistakes to avoid, and how to get a first draft on your iPhone.
  • How to find the key of a songThree ways to find a song's key by ear, a chart of common keys and their main chords, and how to tell major from minor.
  • How to find the BPM of a songThree ways to measure a song's tempo, how to avoid the half-speed and double-speed mix-up, and how to practice at a slower tempo.

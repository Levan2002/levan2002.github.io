Chord Finder

Every chord.
Any song.

Chord Finder shows the chords of a song bar by bar — with its key, tempo (BPM) and sections — from any audio file, video or voice memo on your iPhone. Tap Listen and it names the chord playing around you, live.

What it does

Chords, key and BPM

Major, minor, sevenths, suspended and slash chords, the song's key, tempo and tuning, and its intro, verses, choruses and bridge.

Live Listen

Play or sing near your iPhone and see the chord with how to play it on guitar, ukulele or piano.

Practice tools

Loop any section, slow down to 50% without changing the pitch, transpose to your key and get the easiest capo position.

Private by design

Audio is analyzed on your iPhone with Apple's on-device frameworks. Nothing is uploaded — there's no account and no server.

Chord Finder

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026.

Your audio stays on your iPhone

Chord Finder analyzes songs, videos, voice memos and microphone audio on your device. Recordings and files you add are stored in the app on your iPhone (and in your device backups). They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The microphone is used only while the Listen screen is open.

What we collect

Chord Finder has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not track you.

Purchases of Chord Finder Pro are handled by Apple. To confirm your subscription and restore it on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history (product, price, dates) together with a random identifier created by the app. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and it's used only to provide your purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Chord Finder is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Delete a song in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.

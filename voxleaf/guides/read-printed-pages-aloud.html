Getting printed pages read aloud

Paper doesn’t come with a play button. With the camera and on-device text recognition, an iPhone can turn a printed page into speech in a few seconds: a chapter of a library book, a worksheet, a letter from the bank.

Photograph or scan

In Voxleaf tap +, then Scan pages to use the document scanner, which finds the page edges and flattens the image, or Photos to pick pictures you already took. You can add several pages at once; they are read in order.

Getting clean text

  • Light: even daylight beats a lamp from one side. Avoid the shadow of your phone and hand.
  • Flat pages: press a book open near the spine. Curved lines near the gutter are the most common source of errors.
  • Fill the frame: one page per photo, parallel to the phone.
  • Columns: newspapers and textbooks with two columns are fine; each column is read top to bottom before the next.

What happens to the photos

The text is read from the images on the iPhone itself, and only the text is kept in your library. Nothing is uploaded for recognition.

Handwriting

Neat print handwriting is often recognised, joined-up handwriting much less reliably. Typed and printed text gives the best results.

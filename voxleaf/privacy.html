Privacy

Effective 6 October 2026

Voxleaf is built so that what you read stays yours. Here is exactly what happens to it.

Your documents

Files, links, photos and text you add are turned into plain text and stored inside the app on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to me or to anyone else. Speech is produced on the device too: Apple’s voices by iOS, and Natural Voices by a voice model that ships inside the app. Your text is never sent to a speech service.

Web links

When you import a link, your iPhone downloads that page directly from its website, the way Safari would. That website sees an ordinary request from your device. Voxleaf does not route the request through any server of its own; it has none.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan or pick pages. Text recognition runs on your iPhone, and the images are not stored after the text is read from them.

Purchases

Payments are made through Apple; I never see your card or your name. To know whether Natural Voices is unlocked on your devices, Voxleaf uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and your App Store purchase records. RevenueCat’s policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What is not there

Voxleaf contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking code, and no sign-in. It does not use your location or contacts.

Children

Voxleaf does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Any page, read aloud.

The keeper’s logbook records storms and shipwrecks. On one quiet evening in 1911 he wrote a single line: “Saw the comet again. Told no one.” Nobody on the island knew what he meant.

That is what reading looks like in Voxleaf: the sentence being spoken is shaded and each word lights up as you hear it.

What you can give it

PDF
Reports, papers and scanned documents. Running headers, footers and page numbers are skipped, words split across lines are joined, and the PDF outline becomes chapters.
EPUB
Books without DRM, with their table of contents.
Word and text
.docx, plain text, RTF and HTML files. Word headings become chapters.
Web links
Paste an article link; Voxleaf keeps the article and leaves out menus and banners.
Paper
Photograph or scan printed pages. The text is recognised on your iPhone, two-column layouts included.
Anything else
Copy text from any app and paste it in.

How listening works

  1. Tap the + button in the library and choose where the text comes from.
  2. Press the coral play button. Reading carries on with the screen locked, and the Lock Screen and headphone controls work.
  3. Set the speed anywhere from 0.5× to 3×, skip 15 seconds either way, open the chapter list, or double-tap a paragraph to start there.
  4. Stop whenever you like. Every document keeps its own place, and the library shows how much is left.

The voices

Apple’s voices come with your iPhone. Voxleaf uses them for free, without a time limit, in every language your iPhone speaks.

Natural Voices are ten voices that sound much closer to a person: eight in American English (Heart, Fenrir, Sarah, Michael, Kore, Puck, Nova and Bella) and two in Castilian Spanish (Dora and Alex). They are produced on the iPhone itself by Kokoro, an open neural voice model, so they work in flight mode and your text never leaves the device to be voiced. With Natural Voices you can also save a document as an M4A audio file.

Natural Voices is a yearly subscription with a one-week free trial, or a one-time purchase. You can hear every voice before you decide.

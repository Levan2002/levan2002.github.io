Help

Answers to the questions people ask most. If yours isn’t here, write to levan.topchishvili@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version; a sample file helps too.

A PDF reads nothing, or only a few words.

Some PDFs are pictures of pages with no text layer. Voxleaf notices this and recognises the text from the page images instead, which takes a little longer on long files. If a PDF is password protected, open it in Files first and save an unprotected copy.

An EPUB says it is protected.

Books bought from most stores are locked with DRM, and only the store’s own app can open them. Voxleaf reads DRM-free EPUB files, such as those from Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks or many independent publishers.

A web link gives me the wrong text.

Voxleaf looks for the main article on the page. Pages that load their text with scripts after opening, or that sit behind a login or paywall, can’t be read from the link. Select the text in Safari, copy it and use Paste text instead.

How do I start reading from a particular place?

Double-tap a paragraph, or press and hold it and choose Read from here. The chapter button at the top lists the chapters of books, PDFs with an outline and Word files with headings.

The voice pronounces a word oddly.

Natural voices guess the pronunciation of unusual names and new words. Please send the word and the sentence it was in; it helps improve the next version.

Can it read in other languages?

Yes. Voxleaf detects the language of each document and picks a matching Apple voice. You can change the language in the reader’s menu. The natural voices are available in English and Spanish.

Does it keep reading when I lock the phone?

Yes. Use the Lock Screen, Control Center or your headphones to pause and skip. The sleep timer in the reader menu can stop after a set time or at the end of the chapter.

How do I save a document as audio?

With Natural Voices, open the document, tap the menu at the top right and choose Save as audio. You get an M4A file that you can keep in Files or share.

I bought Natural Voices on another iPhone.

Open Settings in Voxleaf and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Voxleaf. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my documents kept?

Inside Voxleaf on your iPhone. They are included in your normal iPhone backups. Deleting a document from the library removes its text from the device.

Privacy Policy

Heirloom: Photo Album Scanner · Last updated 30 September 2026

In short: your photos never leave your iPhone. We don't collect your name, email or any personal details; purchases are recorded under a random ID only. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Photos and scans

Photos you take or import are processed entirely on your device with Apple's Vision and Core Image frameworks: finding the prints, straightening them, removing glare, restoring color and reading text on the back. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Saved scans are stored in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete any scan in the app; deleting the app deletes them all. If you use iCloud or device backups, they may be included in your own backups under Apple's terms.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used only to scan prints. The app can only add photos to your library (to save your scans, dated when they were taken); it cannot read your library. When you import a photo, iOS gives the app only the photo you selected.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to your purchase.

To keep track of purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. When you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives the App Store purchase record (what was bought, when, and the price) together with a random identifier that the app creates on your device. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and RevenueCat never receives your photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data we collect

Only the purchase history and random identifier described above, used to provide what you bought (app functionality). They are not linked to your identity and not used for tracking. The app contains no analytics or advertising, and apart from purchases it makes no network requests.

Children

Heirloom is suitable for all ages and collects no data about anyone in your photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

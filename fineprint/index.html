Fineprint

Read the fine print on food, in plain words.

Scan a barcode on your iPhone and Fineprint shows what’s in the product, which additives it uses and what they are, how processed it is, and whether it fits the rules you choose. You decide what matters; Fineprint shows the facts and the exact words that matched.

Fineprint shows facts and checks them against rules you set. It isn’t medical, allergy or dietary advice. Always read the pack.

Your rules, not our score

There’s no mystery number. Pick what you avoid and every product is checked against your list. When a rule matches, Fineprint highlights the words in the ingredient list that caused it, so you can see the reason for yourself.

No barcode match? Read the label

If a product isn’t in the database, take a photo of its ingredient list. Fineprint reads the text on your iPhone with Apple’s Vision framework and checks it against your rules and its additive guide. The photo is never uploaded. Labels in English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese, Korean and Chinese are supported.

Free, and Pro

  • FreeUnlimited scans and searches, every fact on every product, label reading, three rules of your choice, offline history
  • ProAll 39 rules at once, family profiles, similar products that fit your rules, compare two products, log a serving to Apple Health

Pro is available monthly, yearly or as a one-time lifetime purchase. There’s no free trial that turns into a charge. See the Terms of Use for details.

Private by design

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • History, saved products, rules and family profiles stay on your iPhone.
  • Only the barcode or search text goes to Open Food Facts to look up a product.

Where the data comes from

Product information comes from Open Food Facts, a free, open food database built by volunteers and run by a non-profit. The database is available under the Open Database License (ODbL), and product images under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA). Nutri-Score and NOVA values are Open Food Facts’ calculations. If something is missing or out of date, anyone can add or correct it on openfoodfacts.org.

The free version

Fineprint is free to download. Without paying you get unlimited barcode scans and searches, every fact Fineprint shows for every product, label reading, up to three rules of your choice, and a history that works offline. The free version isn’t a trial and doesn’t expire.

Fineprint Pro

Fineprint Pro is an optional in-app purchase. It adds all 39 rules at once, family profiles, similar products that fit your rules, side-by-side comparison of two products, and logging a serving’s nutrition to Apple Health. Pro is offered as:

  • Monthly subscription$3.99 per month
  • Yearly subscription$19.99 per year
  • Lifetime, one-time purchase$39.99

Prices are listed in US dollars. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you confirm, and that price is the one that applies. There’s no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Apple processes all payments and handles refunds under its own policies.

Subscriptions

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.

To manage or cancel, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap Manage subscription in Fineprint’s settings. After you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve already paid for. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription.

Lifetime purchase

Lifetime is a one-time, non-renewing purchase. You pay once and Pro stays unlocked for as long as you use Fineprint on a supported device; there’s nothing to renew or cancel. It can be shared with your Family Sharing group. On a new device, tap Restore purchases in Fineprint’s settings.

Not medical advice

Fineprint presents information about products, such as ingredients, additives and their regulatory status, Nutri-Score, NOVA group and nutrition values, and checks it against rules that you choose. It doesn’t judge whether a product is right for you. It isn’t medical, nutritional, allergy or dietary advice, and it doesn’t replace a doctor, dietitian or other qualified professional.

A product that “fits your rules” only means Fineprint didn’t find a match in the information available. Ingredient lists can be incomplete, recipes change, and label reading can miss or misread words. Always read the packaging, especially if you have an allergy or another medical condition.

Neither Open Food Facts nor I can guarantee that this data is complete, accurate or current. Entries are added by contributors and may contain errors or differ from the product in your hand. If you spot a mistake, you can correct it on openfoodfacts.org. Fineprint’s own additive descriptions and regulatory notes are a summary for general information and may lag behind changes in the rules.

Siteward guides

Short, practical guides for anyone who walks a building and has to write down what's wrong: contractors, buyers of new-build homes, landlords and tenants. Each one is written to be useful on its own, and ends with how to do the same thing in Siteward, an iPhone app that turns photos, markup and your voice into a PDF report.

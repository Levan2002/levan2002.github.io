Backfollow is an iPhone app that reads the data export Instagram and TikTok give every user and works out your followers and following lists entirely on your iPhone. No login, no password, no account, nothing uploaded.

Your data stays on your iPhone. Backfollow never asks for your Instagram or TikTok login. You download your own data from the official apps, open the file in Backfollow, and the app reads it on the device. There is no Backfollow account and no server that receives your lists.

What it shows

  • Not following you back: accounts you follow that don't follow you.
  • Fans: accounts that follow you that you don't follow back.
  • Mutuals, plus pending follow requests you sent, accounts you recently unfollowed, close friends and following since dates, where your export includes them.
  • Changes between imports: each new import is compared with the last one, so you see who unfollowed you and who followed you since then.
  • Review queue: go through non-followers one by one and open each profile in the Instagram or TikTok app to unfollow it yourself. Backfollow doesn't automate anything.
  • Keep list for accounts you want to follow no matter what, CSV export, and a share card with your stats (never names).
  • Multiple accounts, kept separate.

How it works

  1. Request your data from Instagram or TikTok (the help page has the exact steps).
  2. Download the file on your iPhone, then choose Share → Backfollow or tap Import in the app.
  3. Read your lists. Come back after the next export to see what changed.

Free and Pro

Free: your counts and the first 10 names of every list. Backfollow Pro unlocks full lists, history between imports, the review queue, multiple accounts, CSV export and share cards without the Backfollow mark.

Pro is $3.99 per month, $19.99 per year (both auto-renewing subscriptions) or $29.99 once for lifetime. Purchases go through Apple, and you can cancel in your Apple Account settings.

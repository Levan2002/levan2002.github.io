Using Backfollow

Backfollow is a tool for reading data that you downloaded from your own accounts. You are responsible for following the terms of the services you use, and for any decision you make about who to follow or unfollow. Backfollow doesn't log in to, automate or change any social account; any unfollowing is done by you in the Instagram or TikTok app.

Help & FAQ

Everything about exporting your data, importing it into Backfollow, and what to expect from the lists.

Contact and support: open an issue on GitHub. Please don't post private account data or your export files there.

How do I export my data from Instagram?

Instagram calls it "Download your information". Menu names change from time to time, but the path is currently:

  1. In the Instagram app, open your profile, tap the menu (☰) and go to Accounts Center.
  2. Tap Your information and permissions, then Download your information.
  3. Tap Download or transfer information and choose the Instagram account you want to check.
  4. Choose Some of your information. Under Connections, tick Followers and following. This is the fastest option, because the export is tiny.
  5. Choose Download to device. Set the date range to All time and the format to JSON (recommended). HTML also works.
  6. Tap Create files. Instagram will notify you and email you when the file is ready, usually within minutes to a few hours.
  7. Open the notification or email on your iPhone, tap the download link, sign in to Instagram in the browser if asked, and download the ZIP file. It is saved in the Files app.
  8. Open the ZIP in Backfollow: tap the file in Files, then Share → Backfollow, or open Backfollow and tap Import. You don't need to unzip it.

The download link is available for a limited time, so download it soon after it arrives. There is a longer walkthrough in the Instagram export guide.

How do I export my data from TikTok?

  1. In the TikTok app, open your profile, tap the menu (☰) and go to Settings and privacy.
  2. Tap Account, then Download your data.
  3. On the Request data tab, choose the file format: JSON or TXT (both work with Backfollow).
  4. Tap Request data. TikTok prepares the file in the background, which can take a few days.
  5. Come back to the same screen and open the Download data tab. When the file is ready, tap Download and save it to your iPhone. Download it soon, because the link expires.
  6. Open the file in Backfollow with Share → Backfollow or the Import button.

TikTok's own menus vary by app version and region, so if a label differs slightly, look for "Download your data" under Account. The TikTok guide explains how to find unfollowers once you have two exports.

Why doesn't Backfollow ask me to log in?

Because it doesn't need to, and because handing your Instagram or TikTok password to a third-party app is a real risk: the app (or whoever runs it) could use your account, and platforms can lock accounts that are accessed by unofficial tools. Backfollow only reads the file you downloaded from the official app. It has no way to sign in as you, post, follow or unfollow.

Why do the lists differ from what I see in Instagram or TikTok?

The export is a snapshot from the moment you requested it. If people followed or unfollowed since then, the lists are out of date. Other reasons for small differences: accounts that were deactivated, deleted or banned may not appear, or may appear in one list and not the other; and private or renamed accounts can show up under their old username. Request a fresh export and import it for the most current picture.

How does history (who unfollowed me) work?

Backfollow has no way to watch your account in the background, because it never connects to it. Instead it compares exports. Import your first export to see your starting lists. Later, request a new export, import it, and Backfollow compares it with the previous one to show who unfollowed you and who followed you in between. The more often you import, the finer the history. History between imports is part of Backfollow Pro; the first import and the counts are free.

What is free and what is in Backfollow Pro?

Free: your counts, plus the first 10 names of each list. Backfollow Pro unlocks full lists, history between imports, the review queue, multiple accounts, CSV export and share cards without the Backfollow mark. Pro is $3.99 per month, $19.99 per year (auto-renewing subscriptions) or $29.99 once for lifetime.

What does the review queue do? Does it unfollow people for me?

No. The review queue shows non-followers one at a time so you can decide quickly. For each one you can keep them (adding them to your keep list) or open their profile in the Instagram or TikTok app and unfollow manually. Backfollow doesn't automate follows or unfollows.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Backfollow's settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. Subscriptions are managed in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Where is my data stored? Is anything uploaded?

Everything stays on your iPhone. The export is read on the device and nothing is uploaded to me or anyone else. See the Privacy Policy. The only outside service is RevenueCat, which validates purchases with an anonymous ID.

How do I delete my data?

In Backfollow go to Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. Also delete the downloaded export from the Files app if you no longer need it, since it is a copy of your followers and following lists.

The import doesn't work. What can I check?

  • Make sure the export includes Followers and following. A partial export of other categories (messages only, for example) won't contain the lists.
  • For Instagram, check that the date range was All time. A shorter range can leave out older followers.
  • Use the original ZIP or the JSON/HTML files from the export. Files that were edited or re-saved by other apps may not be recognised.
  • Still stuck? Open an issue on the support page and describe what you see. Don't attach your real export or any private data.

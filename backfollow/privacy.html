Privacy Policy

Backfollow · updated 5 October 2026

Short version: I, the developer, collect no data. Backfollow reads your Instagram or TikTok export on your iPhone and nowhere else. There is no login, no account, no advertising and no analytics. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.

Your export files and lists

When you import a data export from Instagram or TikTok, Backfollow reads the file on your device only. The files, the lists calculated from them, your keep list, notes of what you reviewed and the history between imports are stored in Backfollow's private storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to me or to any other server, and it is not shared with anyone. Backfollow never asks for your Instagram or TikTok username and password and never connects to your social accounts.

Because the app has no account system, there is nothing on my side to look up, export or delete. I never receive your data in the first place.

What I collect

Nothing. The developer collects no personal data through Backfollow. There are no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and no tracking, and your data is not shared with third parties for any purpose.

Purchases

Backfollow Pro is sold through the App Store as an in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment and I never see your payment details. To validate purchases and unlock Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives your purchase history for Backfollow together with an anonymous app user ID generated for your installation. It is used only for app functionality (confirming that you own Pro and restoring it), it is not linked to your identity, and it is not used for tracking. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in its own privacy policy.

In App Store terms, the data involved is: Purchases and an anonymous user ID, used for App Functionality, not linked to you, not used for tracking.

Sharing and exporting

If you export a CSV or create a share card, the file goes through the iOS share sheet to wherever you choose. A CSV can contain account names from your lists, so check where you send it. Share cards show counts only and never include names. What happens to a file after you share it is up to the app or person you share it with.

Children

Backfollow doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It isn't directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Open Backfollow and go to Settings → Delete all data to remove all imported data from your device. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. You can delete the original export files from your Files app or Downloads at any time. Your purchase history stays with Apple and can't be deleted by deleting the app, so you can still restore Pro later.

Not affiliated with Instagram, Meta or TikTok

Backfollow is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Instagram, Meta or TikTok. Instagram, Meta and TikTok are trademarks of their respective owners.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

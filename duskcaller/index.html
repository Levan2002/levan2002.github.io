Help

Joining an every-phone game. Everyone opens Duskcaller (joining is free), taps Join a Game and picks the host's room code. Keep phones close with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, and allow Duskcaller to find devices on your local network when iOS asks.
A richer narrator voice. Download an Enhanced or Premium voice in the Settings app: Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices. Duskcaller uses the best voice installed for your language; you can pick another in Duskcaller's settings.
Restoring Pro. Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

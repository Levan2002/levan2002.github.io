Duskcaller

Privacy Policy

Updated 2 October 2026

In short: Duskcaller has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your players, setups and games stay on your devices.

Players, setups and games

The names you type, your saved setups, custom roles and word packs are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. If you use iCloud Backup they are part of your own encrypted device backup. The developer has no server and cannot see this data.

Every-phone games

When you host or join an every-phone game, Duskcaller uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity to connect phones that are close to each other, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, with encryption. Only what the game needs travels between the phones in that room: each player's name, their own role, night choices and votes. Nothing goes through the internet or to the developer. iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network; the game works without it in one-phone mode.

Narration

The narrator uses Apple's on-device speech synthesis. No audio is recorded and the microphone is never used.

Sharing

When you share a game recap, the app hands a picture to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm your Pro status. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which product, when and its status) and a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device. This data is used only to provide purchases and restores. It is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking and not sold.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete saved setups from the home screen (touch and hold), remove custom roles and packs in their lists, or delete the app to remove everything. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact the developer.

Help & support

How do I format a script? Put each character’s name before their line, like NORA: I’m leaving. Screenplay style (the name on its own line) and HAMLET. To be… work too. Choose your role, and Scene Partner reads the others.

Recite Aloud says my language isn’t available. On-device speech recognition depends on your iPhone and iOS version. Try Settings › General › Keyboard › Dictation languages, or use Type Test and First Letters, which work in every language.

Is anything uploaded? No. Your texts, recordings of your voice and your progress never leave your iPhone. Export a backup any time from Settings.

Restoring a purchase. Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Anything else: email the developer.

Recital: Learn by Heart

Privacy Policy

Updated 1 October 2026

In short: Recital has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your texts and your voice stay on your iPhone.

Your texts and progress

The texts you add, your practice history and your review schedule are stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s private storage. If you use iCloud Backup, they are part of your own encrypted device backup. Backups you export yourself are files you control. The developer has no server and cannot see this data.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you use Recite Aloud or the hands-free Scene Partner, the microphone is used to follow what you say. Speech is recognized on your iPhone with Apple’s on-device speech recognition. Audio is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere, by the app or to the developer.

Camera, photos and files

Pages you scan or photos you choose are read on your iPhone with Apple’s text recognition, only to get their text. Images are not stored or uploaded. Files you import are read on your iPhone.

Reading aloud

Listen and Scene Partner use the voices built into iOS on your iPhone.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm your Pro status. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which product, when, and its status) and a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device. This data is used only to provide purchases and restores. It is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking, and not sold.

Notifications

Review reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a text from its menu, or delete the app to remove everything. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact the developer.

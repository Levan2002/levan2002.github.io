Privacy Policy

❦

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Guest names, parties and votes are stored only on your device.
  • Invitations leave your device only when you share them with the system share sheet.
  • Purchases go through Apple and RevenueCat, using an anonymous app user ID.
  • On-device speech uses Apple’s system voices.

This policy explains what the Last Course app (“Last Course”, “the app”) does with information. The app is made by Levani Topchishvili (“I”, “me”). I built Last Course so that a dinner party stays between you and your guests.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics

You never sign up or log in. The app shows no advertising and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. I do not track you across apps or websites, and I do not build profiles of you or your guests.

Your parties stay on your device

The names you enter for your guests, the cast list, the parties you set up, the notes and the secret votes are stored only on your device. They are not sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes this information from your device. If you use iCloud Backup or other device backups, those backups may include the app’s data, under Apple’s own terms.

Invitations

Last Course creates each guest’s invitation (an image or PDF) on your device. An invitation leaves your device only when you choose to share it with the system share sheet, for example through Messages, AirDrop or Mail. When you do, it is sent by the service you pick, under that service’s own privacy terms. I never receive a copy.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. I never see your name, email address or payment details. To validate purchases and restore them on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (which products you bought, and when). This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. You can read RevenueCat’s privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Narration

The optional narrator uses Apple’s system text-to-speech voices on your device. The story text is spoken by your phone and is not sent anywhere for processing.

Children

Last Course is intended for teens and adults (ages 12 and up). The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security. Headline fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its effective date. If a change ever affects how the app handles your information, I will describe it here before it takes effect.

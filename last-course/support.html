How do I restore my purchases?

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so you never lose them when you switch phones or reinstall the app.

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
  2. Open Last Course and tap the gear icon on the home screen to open Settings.
  3. Under Cases, tap Restore purchases. You will also find this button at the bottom of the purchase screen.

If a case is still locked, check your internet connection and try again. If it still won’t unlock, email me and I’ll help.

How do I get a better narrator voice?

The narrator uses Apple’s voices built into your iPhone, and it automatically picks the best voice installed for the case’s language. The higher-quality voices are a free download:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.
  3. Choose your language, pick a voice, and download its Enhanced or Premium version.

Back in Last Course, open Settings and tap Hear a sample. You can also change the speaking pace and turn automatic reading on or off there. If you prefer to read the acts aloud yourself, simply leave the narrator off.

Questions

Do my guests need the app?

No. Only the host needs Last Course. Guests receive their invitation as an image or PDF, and on the night the host’s phone is passed around the table for private notes and secret votes.

How do I send invitations?

Cast each guest in the app, then share their invitation with the share button. Choose Messages, AirDrop, Mail or any app on the share sheet, and send it as an image or a PDF. Each invitation includes the character, costume ideas and a sealed secret brief. Send each guest only their own invitation.

How many guests can play?

Six to twelve. Murder on the Tasting Menu is written for 6–8 guests, and the other cases for 6–12. Six core characters are always cast, and extras are optional.

Can the host play too?

Yes. The solution stays hidden until the reveal, so the host can take a character and play along. Just don’t read your guests’ secret briefs before the night.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The evening runs entirely on your phone, including narration. You only need a connection to buy or restore purchases, and to send invitations by message.

Is it suitable for children?

Last Course is written for teens and adults, ages 12 and up. The murders happen off stage, with no gore.

How do I cancel Host Pass?

Host Pass is managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Last Course and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the year you have paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What’s the difference between Host Pass and Every Case Forever?

Host Pass is $29.99 a year and unlocks every case, plus new ones, for as long as it’s active. Every Case Forever is a single $49.99 payment that unlocks every current and future case permanently. You can also buy any single case for $9.99, once.

Which languages are available?

English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian and Japanese.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Read the full Privacy Policy.
